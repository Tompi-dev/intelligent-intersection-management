What this demonstration verifies

  • Queue-dependent phase decisions after minimum green
  • Yellow and all-red stages before opposing green
  • Fixed-time fallback for missing or stale observations
  • Replayable input and output records exported as JSON

What still needs an experiment

Vehicle detection accuracy, queue estimation error, latency, mean waiting time and traffic throughput. A fixed-time versus adaptive transport comparison requires a simulator, matched demand and repeated runs.
