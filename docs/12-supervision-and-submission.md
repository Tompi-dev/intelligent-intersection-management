# Supervisor review and submission procedure

The supervisor is asked to review whether the single-junction scope has sufficient engineering complexity, whether stakeholder evidence supports the requirements, whether timing and phase assumptions fit the test geometry, and whether the evaluation can establish the stated aim.

Provide the proposal, literature synthesis, requirements, three diagrams, prototype and verification plan together. Record the actual review date, document version, substantive comment, owner, planned revision and link to the change. Do not fill the feedback journal with invented approval or an assumed supervisor identity.

Before submission confirm team identities, the supervisor, repository and YouTrack links, dataset permission, actual stakeholder evidence and review outcomes. Complete the submission record with the exact commit, immutable stage tag, completed work, limitations and outstanding tasks. Use pre-defense-1 for the first stage, pre-defense-2 for the second and final-pre-defense for the final checkpoint. Do not create a submission tag until the intended reviewed version is selected.

A complete application is not required at this checkpoint. The defensible submission must nevertheless distinguish prepared drafts and prototype checks from verified user research, empirical results and actual review. Current organisational gaps are tracked in OPEN_ITEMS.md rather than represented as completed work.
